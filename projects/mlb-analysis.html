PYTHON · PANDAS · DATA ANALYSIS

MLB payroll & performance.

Analyzing the relationship between MLB payroll spending and team performance.

ORIGINAL ANALYSIS / 2023 MLB DATA
01 / Question

Can money buy wins?

I studied whether team payroll predicted regular-season performance in the 2023 MLB season, and whether the highest-spending teams consistently converted spending into wins.

02 / Method

Combining three datasets.

I used Python and Pandas to clean and integrate payroll data from CSV, 2023 standings from a saved HTML table, and metropolitan population data. I compared payroll tiers, charted win percentage, and examined the role of market size.

03 / Findings

Spending helps. It doesn't guarantee success.

The overall correlation between payroll and win percentage was approximately 0.20, a weak positive relationship. High-payroll teams averaged roughly 0.54 win percentage, compared with approximately 0.48 in the other payroll tiers, but the results overlapped substantially.

04 / Perspective

Market size adds context.

In this analysis, the correlation between the population measure and payroll was approximately 0.71. That suggests larger markets tended to spend more, while payroll alone explained relatively little of the variation in wins. This is a one-season analysis, not proof of causation.
