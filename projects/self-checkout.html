Self-checkout research.

Understanding common frustrations in grocery self-checkout experiences.

Authentic project materials onlySELF-CHECKOUT
Original photos, diagrams, screenshots, or visualizations will be added here. No AI-generated imagery is used.
01 / Context

Overview

I explored the self-checkout experience through survey research, interviews, and in-store observations, looking at the friction shoppers encounter while scanning, bagging, and paying.

02 / Process

Research & methods

Surveys using Qualtrics, four interviews, five observations, and qualitative synthesis of recurring issues.

03 / Analysis

What I explored

Research topics included scanning problems, assistance requests, produce lookup, payment flows, and limited bagging space. These are areas studied, not claims of measured prevalence.

04 / Next

Deliverables & updates

The case study will be updated with original observation notes, survey summaries, personas, and any prototype or redesigned interface produced for the course.
