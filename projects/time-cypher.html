ARDUINO · PROTOTYPING · COMPLETED PROJECT

Time Cypher.

A hands-on alarm clock prototype that turns waking up into a logic challenge.

THE BUILT PROTOTYPE / ENGINEERING EXPO
01 / The problem

Make waking up a little less automatic.

Time Cypher is a puzzle-based alarm clock designed to engage the user in a short challenge rather than simply tapping snooze. The project targeted the difficulty many people have waking up in the morning.

02 / Building it

From CAD to working prototype.

Our engineering design project brought together a physical enclosure, four illuminated tactile buttons, a digital time display, sound output, and a programmed puzzle interaction. The final prototype was displayed alongside our CAD model, flowchart, requirements, and planned improvements at an engineering expo.

03 / Design decisions

Interaction you can feel.

Rather than relying on a touchscreen, the device uses four colorful arrow buttons and a compact display. The design communicates the available inputs physically, while the internal logic determines the puzzle sequence.

04 / Looking ahead

What we would improve.

Our expo poster identified potential next steps such as Bluetooth connectivity, sleep tracking, app connectivity, and a smaller enclosure. This prototype gave our team a tangible foundation for refining both functionality and usability.
